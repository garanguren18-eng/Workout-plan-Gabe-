💡 Weight Progression Rule

If you can easily complete all sets at the top rep range with perfect form, increase the dumbbell weight by 5 lbs in your next session. When you reach 50 lbs on DBs, increase time-under-tension by lowering the weight for 3 seconds per rep or wrapping a resistance band around your hands.

Month 1: Foundation & Form Mastership

Focus: Build core strength, perfect form, establish movement patterns. Controlled 2-sec down, 1-sec up tempo.

Tuesday — Upper Body & Cardio
🏃 Finisher: 15 Mins Stationary Bike (Moderate Pace)
Dumbbell Bench Press
3 × 10–12 30–35 lbs DBs
Form Notes: Chest focus; keep shoulders down and tucked.
Bent-Over DB Rows
3 × 10–12 30–35 lbs DBs
Form Notes: Flat back, pull dumbbells towards hip.
Standing DB Shoulder Press
3 × 10–12 20–25 lbs DBs
Form Notes: Core tight, don't arch back.
Band Lat Pulldown / Pull-Apart
3 × 15 Medium Band
Form Notes: Squeeze shoulder blades together for 1 sec at peak contraction.
DB Curls / Overhead Triceps
3 × 12 20lb DBs / 25lb KB
Form Notes: Controlled negative tempo on the lowering portion.
Thursday — Lower Body & Core
KB Goblet Squats
3 × 12–15 25lb KB / 30lb DB
Form Notes: Deep depth, elbows inside knees at the bottom.
DB Bulgarian Split Squats
3 × 10/leg 15–20 lbs DBs
Form Notes: Rear foot elevated on bench; maintain balance.
DB Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs)
3 × 12 35–40 lbs DBs
Form Notes: Hinge at hips, slight knee bend, stretch hamstrings.
Band Glute Bridges
3 × 15–20 Heavy Band
Form Notes: Band around knees; squeeze glutes at top for 2 secs.
KB Swings
3 × 15 25 lb KB
Form Notes: Explosive hip drive, hinge not squat.
Plank & Bicycle Crunch Circuit
3 Rounds Bodyweight
Form Notes: 45s Plank + 20 Bicycle Crunches back-to-back.
Sunday — Full Body & Cardio
🏃 Treadmill Incline: 20 Mins (10–12% incline, 3.0 mph)
DB Thrusters (Squat to Overhead)
3 × 10 20–25 lbs DBs
Form Notes: Fluid movement using leg power to drive DBs overhead.
Single-Arm KB Rows
3 × 12/side 25lb KB / 30lb DB
Form Notes: Hand braced on bench, pull elbow to hip.
DB Push-ups (Hands on DBs)
3 × Max Bodyweight
Form Notes: Full chest depth, neutral wrists on handles.
Band Deadlifts / Monster Walks
3 × 15 Heavy Band
Form Notes: Keep constant tension on glutes throughout movement.

Month 2: Hypertrophy & Progressive Weight Overload

Focus: Increase working weight by 5–10 lbs across movements. Keep rest intervals strictly to 75 seconds.

Tuesday — Upper Body & Cardio
🏃 Finisher: 20 Mins Bike (+1 Resistance Level)
DB Bench Press / Bent Rows
3 × 10–12 40–45 lbs DBs
Progression: +5 to 10 lbs increase from Month 1!
Standing DB Overhead Press
3 × 10–12 25–30 lbs DBs
Progression: Maintain strict form with increased weight.
Bicep Curls / Triceps Extensions
3 × 12 25 lbs DBs
Progression: Increased curl load; follow with bike cardio.
Thursday — Lower Body & Core
DB Goblet Squats
3 × 12 35–40 lb DB
Progression: Switch from 25lb KB to heavier DB held at chest.
DB RDLs & Split Squats
3 × 12 45–50 lb RDL / 25 lb Split
Progression: Near max capacity weight for RDLs.
KB Swings & Core Circuit
3 × 20 / 3 Rds 25 lb KB / 60s Plank
Progression: Swings increased to 20 reps; Plank extended to 60s.
Sunday — Full Body & Cardio
🏃 Treadmill Interval Finisher: 20 Mins (12% incline @ 3.2 mph)
DB Thrusters & Single-Arm Row
3 × 12 25lb Thrust / 35lb Row
Progression: Pace briskly between sets.
DB Push-ups & Band Deadlifts
3 × Max / 15 BW + Heavy Band
Progression: Aim for +2 reps per push-up set over M1.

Month 3: Maximum Intensity, Band Resistance & HIIT

Focus: Maximize dumbbell load (up to 50 lbs). Add band resistance to DB exercises to challenge top range of motion. Rest 60s.

Tuesday — Upper Body & Cardio
🏃 Bike HIIT: 15 Mins (30s Sprint / 30s Easy)
DB Bench Press (+ Loop Band)
4 × 10–12 45–50 lbs DBs
Peak Tension: Add resistance band behind back for top lock-out resistance.
Bent DB Rows & Shoulder Press
4 × 12 50lb Row / 30-35lb Press
Peak Capacity: Max equipment threshold (50 lb dumbbells).
Curls / Overhead Ext / Bike HIIT
4 × 12 25–30 lbs DBs
Intensity: 4 heavy sets, immediately followed by 15-min Bike HIIT.
Thursday — Lower Body & Core
DB Goblet Squats (2s Pause)
4 × 12–15 45–50 lb DB
Tempo: Pause 2 seconds at bottom of squat before driving up.
DB RDLs & DB Split Squats
4 × 12 50lb RDL / 25-30lb Split
Focus: Deep hamstring stretch & glute squeeze at top.
KB Swings & Core Finisher
4 × 20 / 4 Rds 25lb KB / Plank+Leg Lift
Burnout: High-density core burning with plank + leg lifts.
Sunday — Full Body & Cardio
🏃 Treadmill HIIT: 15 Mins (1 Min Run @ 6–7 mph / 1 Min Walk)
DB Thrusters & Single Arm Rows
4 × 12 25-30lb Thrust / 40-45lb Row
Effort: Maximum effort full-body compound movement.
Band-Resisted Push-Ups
4 × Max Bodyweight + Band
Power: Resistance band across back/shoulders for chest power.
